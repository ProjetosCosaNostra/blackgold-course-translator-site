Compatibility and limitations

Compatibility and limitations

The historical 0.6.0 build targets Udemy learning pages on desktop Chromium browsers. This website works on mobile; that does not mean the extension works in mobile browsers. Translation support varies by browser version, language and availability of local models.

An installation link will appear only after actual validation and publication in an extension store.
