BlackGold Course Translator • 0.6.0

Privacy Policy

October 2, 2026

This policy describes the documented 0.6.0 implementation; later releases require renewed verification.

1. Scope

This policy describes how the BlackGold Course Translator extension handles data. It was designed to translate captions and transcripts from supported courses in the browser and keep translated captions in sync with the lesson.

2. Data accessed

On compatible pages, the extension may locally access visible caption and transcript text; course and page titles; technical lesson identifiers present in the URL or page; the video state required for synchronization; the selected target language; and the panel visibility preference. Version 0.6.0 targets Udemy learning pages matching its manifest.

3. Local storage

The extension uses chrome.storage.local to save the target language and panel visibility preferences. These preferences stay in the browser until changed, cleared or removed with the extension.

4. Translation and processing

Version 0.6.0 uses translation features provided by a compatible browser. The audit of its release candidate found no integration with a developer-operated translation server and no fetch, XMLHttpRequest, WebSocket, sendBeacon calls or known first-party analytics systems. This statement is limited to the audited build.

5. Data transmission

Version 0.6.0 was not designed to send captions, transcripts, browsing history, personal identifiers, credentials, financial or health data, or precise location to the developer. The extension does not sell user data.

6. Clipboard

The “Copy status” command places technical session diagnostics in your local clipboard only when you activate it. You decide whether to share that information.

7. Third parties

Course platforms retain their own terms and policies. The extension does not grant course access, replace authentication or bypass DRM or access restrictions.

8. Security and data minimization

Version 0.6.0 declares storage and activeTab permissions and limits its content script to the Udemy learning-page pattern in its manifest. Future permissions and compatibility must be audited again.

9. Policy changes

This policy will be updated when new functionality, permissions, supported platforms or translation methods materially change data handling.

10. Contact
